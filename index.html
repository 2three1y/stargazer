        Keyboard shortcuts

        These work anywhere except inside a text box or menu.

        • Right or Left arrow, or J and K: next or previous star
        • Down or Up arrow: next or previous group
        • D: details for this star
        • Space or P: play this star again
        • S: start or stop a sweep
        • Slash: jump to Find
        • Escape: stop sounds and close details
